The Strategy

The Framework Behind a 91.3% Win Rate

That is what Benicio held through the month of May trading this. It is different from most things you have traded before, because it stops trying to predict the market and starts reacting to it. Four rules do the heavy lifting.

01

Conditions, not daily bias

The market moves both ways almost every session. Instead of picking a direction at the open, you set "if this, then that" conditions on fair value gaps, rejection blocks, and equilibrium, then trade whichever side the market confirms.

02

Continuations only

No reversals. You take trades with the trend. Same entry model, but trading continuation instead of trying to catch tops and bottoms is worth a 10 to 15% jump in win rate on its own.

03

Equilibrium plus confluence

Every entry is lined up with the equilibrium of the range, the 0.5 on the Fibonacci tool. A fair value gap sitting on equilibrium is two confluences instead of one, and that is what filters out the entries that run straight to your stop.

04

Conservative targets

No swinging for a $5,000 screenshot. You take your piece of the pie, the internal liquidity, and log the green day. Stacking $250 to $500 winners is what actually gets prop payouts approved.

Why Most Beginners Fail

If you have traded before and walked away thinking you are just bad at it, read this carefully. Most beginners who work a job do not fail because the strategy is wrong. They fail because of when and how they take the trade.

The Losing Pattern

  • Rushed prep before work, then a trade taken at 9:47 because a meeting starts at 10
  • Collecting strategies instead of mastering one read
  • Jumping between futures, options and crypto, a little of everything, never enough of anything
  • Buying course after course, watching alone, asking in a dead chat, then quitting
  • No fixed risk, so one red trade erases a good week

What Consistent Traders Do

  • Trade only inside the window where they have full time and full attention
  • Run one framework, the same way, every session, until the read is automatic
  • Trade with live guidance, not alone with a video library
  • Set the stop and the size before entering, so losses stay small and wins stay bigger
  • Bring every trade to review and fix the pattern, not the indicator
The strategy is usually fine. The timeline and the execution are what break it. Beginners rush: pass the account today, first payout this week. Benicio's fix is the opposite. Extend the timeline, trade only the windows where you have full attention, and let consistency compound. The people who give themselves the grace of time tend to hit their goals sooner, not later.
