The Story

From A 15-Year-Old Watching Charts To Running Aval Trades

Benicio Valentin — known online as AvalTrades — started day trading at 15. Most people that age are figuring out a part-time job. He was figuring out order flow.

He built his name in public, documenting live account flips and futures payouts through prop firms like Apex Trader Funding, and a trading lifestyle that started years before most traders even open their first chart. That transparency — winning trades, losing trades, real accounts, real numbers — is what grew his following on Instagram and YouTube.

That same transparency is what became Aval Trades: a day trading brand, mentorship community, and educational resource built around futures trading, market psychology, and liquidity concepts — not signals, not guesswork.

Trading Style

How Benicio Actually Trades

His models pull heavily from institutional concepts — the same ideas behind ICT-style methodology.

CONCEPTS

Liquidity pools, session highs and lows, order flow, and change of state delivery — the institutional framework his models are built around.

MARKETS

Prefers high-reward, major market-open setups in futures — where liquidity and volatility create the clearest opportunities.

EXECUTION

Teaches students to take consistent "base hits" instead of overleveraging — practical risk management over swinging for home runs.

FORMAT

Live trading sessions and real-time market breakdowns — students watch the framework applied to the market as it happens.
