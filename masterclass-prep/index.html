Now let me answer the top 5 most common questions I get asked before a masterclass like this...

1) Is this just another strategy course?

  • No. Strategy is one of three pillars.
  • The other two, risk management and the session you trade, are why most traders stay stuck even after they find a setup they like.
  • Most people trade on feel. They predict, they hope, they revenge trade after a loss.
  • This is mechanical. Fixed rules for risk, fixed rules for entries, the same way every time.

2) I've blown accounts before. How is this different?

  • Blown accounts almost never come from a bad setup. They come from sizing and from breaking your rules under pressure.
  • I blew every dollar my dad gave me before I figured that out.
  • The risk framework sizes every trade off your worst losing streak, so a rough week is survivable.
  • I'll show you exactly how on the masterclass.

3) I work a full-time job. Can I actually do this?

  • Yes, because I built mine the same way.
  • I trade the Asia session at 8 PM Eastern, after the workday is done.
  • About 2 focused hours a night is enough when you're following fixed rules instead of guessing.
  • Don't quit your job to start this. Build it around your schedule first.

4) Do I need experience or a lot of money?

  • The masterclass is built for traders who are already in the game, or who stepped away after blowing accounts.
  • Brand new? You'll still get value from the framework. Come ready to take notes.
  • With prop firms you trade the firm's capital, not your own savings.
  • And trading always involves risk. What you control is the size of each loss, which is exactly what the risk framework fixes.
